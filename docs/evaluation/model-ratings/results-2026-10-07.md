Quality means satisfying the entire task, including exact formatting. It is not factual accuracy alone: seven of Apple's thirteen failures were format-only, while six involved incorrect or omitted content. Qwen had two format-only failures and three incorrect-content failures. Examples: Apple omitted requested drinking water and applied an ordinary price instead of a stated exception; both models made arithmetic/word-count errors; Apple added prohibited JSON fences, and both added prose to some number-only requests. Every decision is retained in the [rubric grades](grades-2026-10-07.json), based on assistant review rather than independent human review.

[Raw report](paced-results-2026-10-07.json). This is a small development set, previously used on Mac, with one new phone response per case/provider. It is neither held-out accuracy nor a general ranking of the underlying models. No prompt tuning, answer retries, or favorable-answer selection were used within the complete run. Reported timing includes native token preparation, Qwen artifact hashing/loading, and generation; it excludes app database/UI work and inter-response rests. Native generation-only medians were 1.6765 s (Apple) and 2.012 s (Qwen); these are separate observations, not the speed-score inputs. Shipping caps differed: Apple 512 tokens, Qwen 256. Inputs/instructions were identical, and each provider used its production adapter.

## Conditions and retained failed controls

The completed run was unplugged, began at nominal thermal state, used a dark diagnostic screen with system brightness unchanged, and rested ten seconds after every response. All sampled before/after thermal states were nominal or fair; the app remained active and on battery. This does not establish sustained-use stability or attribute whole-device heat to one model.

The [initial burst](burst-control-2026-10-07.json) stopped after five pairs with Qwen interrupted/unavailable. A [nominal-start wait](nominal-readiness-2026-10-07.json) timed out without responses. A [paced warm-start control](paced-warm-control-2026-10-07.json) stopped after five responses: Qwen admission failed at serious thermal state while the app was active and about 3.4 GB of process headroom remained. The owner then explicitly requested cooling and an unplugged retry. Protocol changes were declared before the replacement outputs; all controls remain available and supply no ratings. Production resource guards were never relaxed.

Memory headroom is admission telemetry, not model-attributable memory usage. Apple system-service memory is not measured comparably with Qwen's in-process memory, so neither gets a memory score. This run did not collect calibrated matched energy/idle measurements, so neither gets a battery score. Prior battery and Mac results remain separate historical evidence.

The diagnostic uses fictional data and existing read-only weights, does not open the personal database, and does not change the saved model choice. Its entrypoint and launch flag are separate from production. Photo understanding remains on hold.
