# Local vision feasibility — measured Mac results

**Result: the existing runtime can load the approved image model and answer from pixels, but this setup failed a basic missing-information safety probe. Do not promote it into Sekret's grounded-answer pipeline.** No app integration or iPhone vision test was performed.

## Reproduction and artifacts

Machine identified by runtime as Apple M2; macOS 26.6.1 (25G76). Existing llama.cpp b11429 (`d81235049384534c167caea52b85a694f6103d14`) XCFramework was reused. Its archive SHA-256 was rechecked as `e26a6a0a813f5760fe45834ce239d5b525e4e2442d4fd017221d9f0438383174`. Both approved GGUF sizes/SHA-256 matched [the manifest](artifacts.json) after retrieval; total 545,590,272 bytes. No other weights/runtime downloaded. Final disk check: 5.9 GiB free.

Mac ABI probe compiled/ran before weight retrieval. Read-only `nm` found the corresponding multimodal symbols in the iOS arm64 slice; symbol presence is not evidence of device performance or behavior. The actual vision executable compiled without additional package dependencies, successfully initialized the SmolVLM decoder and its paired projector, and ran `mtmd` image encoding/evaluation.

The GGUF card, publisher card and canonical Apache-2.0 license text are retained under ignored `artifacts/`. Publisher-declared base models were also checked: SmolLM2-360M-Instruct revision `a10cc1512eabd3dde888204e902eca88bddb4951` and SigLIP-base-patch16-512 revision `753a949581523b60257d93e18391e8c27f72eb22` both declare Apache-2.0. This screening is not a full redistribution audit. [SmolLM2 publisher metadata](https://huggingface.co/api/models/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-360M-Instruct), [SigLIP publisher metadata](https://huggingface.co/api/models/google/siglip-base-patch16-512).

## Fixed settings

Each probe was a separate process: one image, one text question, 2048 context, 128 maximum output tokens, greedy decoding, four CPU threads and Metal offload. ImageIO applies orientation, sRGB conversion and white compositing for alpha, caps the longest edge at 1024, then feeds RGB pixels to model-native `mtmd` preprocessing. Publisher's pinned single-user image-then-text prompt subset is used; general chat-template support and reference preprocessing/token parity were not evaluated.

Only repository-owned generated turtle artwork and a fictional notice were used. No stock Photos database traversal or personal images. Raw results/logs are under ignored `results/`; exact responses and key metrics are retained below.

## Quality probes

| Probe | Question | Exact response | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `turtle` | Describe the animal visible in this image. Mention only visible features. | ` Turtles.` | Identifies animal class, but underdescribes the image and uses plural for one turtle. Partial, not a good caption. |
| `document-deadline` | According to this notice, how many calendar days after the final workday must equipment be returned? | ` Equipment must be returned to the fictional Aster Workshop service desk seven calendar days after the final workday.` | Correctly reads the seven-day deadline and visible return location. |

These three development probes are not an accuracy benchmark and were not repeated. They establish actual image processing and one concrete failure, not a population-level error rate. This result applies to this quantized pair, preprocessing, runtime and prompting setup; no full-precision comparison was run.

All finished with an end-of-generation token, not the output cap. Recorded thermal state was `1` (fair). The first run's total includes backend/Metal startup before the model-load timer; shader compilation appears in its log. It is **not** valid to subtract the model-load value and call the remainder image latency. Subsequent processes benefited from OS/file/shader caches, so these are not repeated cold-start measurements.

RSS comes from process `getrusage` high water; physical footprint comes from `task_vm_info.phys_footprint` sampled every 20 ms after hashing. They are different accounting measures; sampled footprint can miss spikes and is not a complete GPU/shared-resource budget. Do not present approximately 309 MB footprint as the phone's required RAM. No isolated-memory baseline, energy study, long-run thermal measurement or iPhone measurement exists.

## Decision and next gate

Stop this bounded stage here. There is enough evidence to say the native route works on Mac, and enough counterevidence to reject shipping it as trusted visual grounding. In particular, caption → text embedding → text verifier would not detect that the caption itself fabricated a visual fact.

The owner's phone is confirmed iOS 27.0, so Apple's native on-device image interface remains a worthwhile comparison **if** compatible toolchain access is separately authorized; current installed SDK 26.5 cannot build it. No Xcode upgrade or new model retrieval was done/authorized by this stage. Coordinate any later iOS vision harness after the separate text-generation phone evaluation; establish held-out visual truth/missing-fact cases, preprocessing parity, admission budgets, cancellation/background privacy and device memory before considering production work.
