## Findings

- Reading clean text, scenes and presence is strong: 17/18, and the only miss was a guardrail refusal, not a misreading. A refusal on an ordinary medicine label would also hit real users.
- The model fails on **partial or tiny text**: it reports whatever fragment it can see ("1", "Data about the") as if it were the answer. v1 had no case like this, so tuning could not catch it.
- Raw counts were again all one too high (now 6/6 wrong across v1 and v2), confirming that declining counts is the right product behaviour.
- Median first token 2.63 s; maximum total 3.32 s.
