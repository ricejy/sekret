## Interpretation

These results establish repeatable development inputs for stronger candidates, not a catalogue recommendation. Differences in quantization, native templates, samplers and preprocessing must remain visible. Do not compare these Mac fresh-process numbers with warmed iPhone results as though hardware were the only changed variable.

Raw reports, prompts, logs and artifact manifests are small and remain after unused downloaded weights are removed at the user's request. Do not delete a shared runtime or an artifact still required by an active evaluation.
